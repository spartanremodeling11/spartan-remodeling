Questions about window prices

How much does window replacement cost in Fort Worth?

With me, $1,300 a window installed when you replace 8 or more, and $1,500 for a single window. That covers the window, removal, install, trim, caulk and haul-off. Second-story windows are $75 more each.

How much do energy-efficient windows cost?

The prices on this page already include double-pane low-E glass with argon gas. My single-hung windows are rated at a 0.28 U-factor and a 0.17 solar heat gain coefficient.

Is the price on this page the real price?

Yes. This is the price list I quote from. The final number is confirmed after a 15-minute measure and my supplier’s quote for your exact sizes.

Does it cost more per window to do just a few?

A little. One window is $1,500, two are $2,800 and three are $4,050. From four windows up, it’s $1,300 a window.

Can I still get a federal tax credit for new windows?

Not for windows installed now. The federal credit ended for windows installed after December 31, 2025.
