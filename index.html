Questions people ask

How long does an install take?

Most homes are done in one day and never take more than two.

What warranty do I get?

A 5-year workmanship warranty from me, plus NT Window’s manufacturer warranty on the windows themselves.

How do I pay?

50% when you sign, so I can order your windows. The other 50% after the install and a room-by-room walkthrough. Take 10% off if you pay cash or check instead of financing.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, financing is available, subject to credit approval. Ask me about it at your measure.

Do second-story windows cost more?

$75 more per upstairs window. The estimator adds it for you.

Who gets permits?

If your city or HOA requires one, the homeowner gets it. I’m glad to tell you what to ask for.

What if my sheetrock gets damaged?

If I damage it during the install, I repair and paint it. Damage or rot that was already there isn’t covered.

How do you seal the windows?

Bostik DUO-SIL caulk to the brick, siding or trim outside and the sheetrock inside. Low-expansion spray foam goes in any gap over 1/8 inch.

Ready to see what your windows would cost?

Price it online, or send me a photo of your windows. I reply between jobs, usually the same day.
