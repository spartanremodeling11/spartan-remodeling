How it works, start to finish

No three-hour sales pitch and no crew you’ve never met. Here’s exactly what happens from your first price to the final walkthrough.

  1. 1

    Price it online, then a 15-minute measure

    • Start with the estimator to see a real price before anyone comes out.
    • I come by, measure every opening to the fraction of an inch, and answer your questions. About 15 minutes, no sales pitch.
  2. 2

    A written quote

    • You get a written quote by text or email with the total price and what’s included. No hidden fees.
    • You can read the warranty details and sample contract before you sign anything.
  3. 3

    Sign and order

    • Sign the agreement and pay the 50% deposit so I can order your custom windows.
    • Once the order is in, I send you a quick video confirming what was ordered and when it should arrive.
  4. 4

    Pick an install day

    • When the windows arrive, we pick a day that works for you.
    • Two days before, I send a short video on how to get ready: clear about 3 feet in front of each window, take down blinds, and keep pets put up.
  5. 5

    Install day and walkthrough

    • I take out the old windows, set the new ones, foam any gap over 1/8 inch, and caulk inside and out with Bostik DUO-SIL.
    • I haul off the old windows and clean up. Most homes are done in one day, never more than two.
    • We walk through every room and test every window together. You pay the final 50% when you’re satisfied.
