Warranty and contract

Read the warranty, the windows I use and my standard contract terms before you ever sign.

5-year workmanship warranty

Backed by me personally. Covers my labor, anchoring, the Bostik DUO-SIL caulking and the installation staying weather-tight for 5 years.

NT Presidential manufacturer warranty

NT Window’s own warranty covers the windows themselves: the vinyl frame, the insulated glass and glass breakage against defects. You get the paperwork at the walkthrough.

The windows I use: NT Presidential

I install vinyl replacement windows from NT Window, built for the Texas climate. My standard recommendation is the NT Presidential 900 series. If you’re on a tight budget I can price other manufacturers, but the warranty coverage changes if you switch.

Custom sizes only. I measure every opening to the fraction of an inch. Please don’t order windows on your own; off-the-shelf windows won’t fit a precise opening and can’t be covered by my workmanship warranty.

Sample contract terms

Every customer gets this agreement, in plain words, before signing.

1. Scope of work

  • Removal: old windows and frames removed; all debris hauled away.
  • Installation: custom windows installed per the manufacturer’s specifications.
  • Insulation: gaps over 1/8 inch sealed with low-expansion spray foam.
  • Finish: Bostik DUO-SIL caulk to brick, siding or trim outside and to sheetrock inside.
  • Permits: the homeowner gets any required city or HOA permits.
  • Sheetrock: if I damage sheetrock during the install, I repair and paint it. Damage, softness or rot that was already there is excluded.

2. Payment

  • Deposit: 50% at signing, to order custom materials and hold your install date.
  • Final: 50% after the install and a successful final walkthrough.

3. Hidden conditions

I’m not responsible for problems hidden inside the walls. If structural rot turns up in studs or headers, work stops in that spot and we talk about a separate estimate.

4. Delays

I’m not liable for delays from supply shortages, manufacturer production schedules or severe weather.
